            The idea

            Let's teach an LLM to be the in-game leader in a Counter-Strike round.

            The in-game leader calls where all five players go.

            They never see the enemy. They call the round from comms and memory.

            So Kimi gets exactly that: what the Counter-Terrorists see and hear, plus a notebook of past rounds.
